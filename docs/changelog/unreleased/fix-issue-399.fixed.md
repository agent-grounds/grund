- [§FS-values.3.1.1.1](../../functional-spec/FS-values.md#3111-a-literal-that-closes-its-line-onto-the-citation): `grund check` now refuses a value binding that a line break splits. The literal closes its line, and its parenthesized citation opens the next line of the same paragraph, blockquote or comment block. The finding is `invalid-value-binding` at the literal, and its message asks to put the two on one line. Before, such a binding was neither compared nor refused, so a binding that a reflow had split passed silently with a wrong literal, while two spaces or a tab in the same place were already refused. A blank line between the two still leaves ordinary prose and an ordinary citation. Closes [issue #399](https://github.com/agent-grounds/grund/issues/399). (PR #TBD)
