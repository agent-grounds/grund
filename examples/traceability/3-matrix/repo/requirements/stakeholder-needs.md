# LC-1 charge controller: stakeholder needs

What the people who use and service the LC-1 need from its charger, in their
words. Each system requirement in [system.md](system.md) traces to one of these.

## STK-001: Charging never overheats the battery

The device is left on charge unattended, overnight, in a warm room or a parked
car. It must never charge the battery while the battery is too hot to charge
safely.

## STK-002: A stopped charger stays safe and says why

When the charger cannot charge safely it stops, stays stopped, and shows that it
has stopped. Service staff can read afterwards why it stopped.
