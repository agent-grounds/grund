# ADR-0005: Claim a seat from the phone

Date: 2026-09-28

## status: Proposed

## context: Context

The door readers fail in cold weather, and a reader that is down takes no
claims, so seats with students in them are released.

## decision: Decision

Each desk carries a printed code. A student claims the seat by scanning it with
the phone they booked on, and the door readers are retired.

## consequences: Consequences

Claims no longer depend on the readers. A student without a phone claims at the
desk.
