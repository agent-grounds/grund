# STEER-product: Product Overview

Quillmarsh Pantry sells dry goods online to home cooks. Checkout is the one
page every order passes through, so a change there is a change to every order.

## shoppers: Who shops here

Home cooks buying for a household, mostly on a phone, often returning for the
same staples. They expect a promotion to work exactly as it was advertised.

## goals: What the shop optimises for

A shopper sees the price they will pay before they pay it, and that price never
changes between the basket and the receipt.
