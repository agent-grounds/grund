# The 1.0 freeze record

This is the audit [#467] asks for before `1.0.0` is cut: one candidate commit, the
two results recorded on it, and for every promise `1.0` makes and every condition
of the release gate a row with its proof and its outcome. It was written once, on
2026-10-31, from `main` as it stood that day, as [§DISC-core-concerns.9.5](../discussions/proposals/2026-09-30-core-concerns.md#95-100) and the
owner's clarification on #467 (comment 6245457368) ask.

**Not every row holds.** Six rows are red (section 5), so this record does not close #467,
and `1.0.0` is not to be cut from the candidate it names. Each red row says why. A row
that waits on the owner's ruling names the comment on #467 that asks for it, and
section 4.7 lists all of them. A later update appends to this file and keeps what is
here (section 7).

Verifying a candidate is not a release. Nothing here bumps a version, tags,
dispatches a release or changes who may publish: the owner dispatches
`release-minor.yml` with `version: 1.0.0` ([§FS-distribution.4.4.2](../functional-spec/FS-distribution.md#442-deliberately-select-the-first-stable-release-from-the-dispatch-main-tip)), and that
choice remains theirs alone.

### 3.1 The Rust API closure, recomputed

[§DISC-core-concerns.10.2](../discussions/proposals/2026-09-30-core-concerns.md#102-the-five-decisions-the-plan-makes-one-way) (item 3) has this audit recompute the closure rather
than copy it. The recomputation reads the candidate's `crates/grund-core/src` as
text. It collects the 275 public root names that `lib.rs` exports and starts from
the 17 entry points and 3 typed refusals that [§FS-distribution.3.1.2](../functional-spec/FS-distribution.md#312-what-10-freezes) names. Then it
follows every root type named in an entry point's signature, a promised struct's
`pub` fields, an enum's variants or a type alias, until nothing new is reached.
Adding the `pub` method signatures of the reached types reaches nothing more.

The result is **118 names**. They are exactly the 118 rows the
[inventory][inventory] marks `frozen at 1.0`, and none of them is
`#[doc(hidden)]`. That leaves 157 hidden rows, and 118 + 157 = 275. The test
constant `FROZEN_COUNT` is 118 too.

The promise itself holds. Its text does not: [§FS-distribution.3.1.2](../functional-spec/FS-distribution.md#312-what-10-freezes) still says
"That is 115 names". Three names became `frozen at 1.0` after the sentence was
written. `FmtCounts` and `FmtLockFile` came in `b2b91d76e5` on 2026-10-26, and
`ValueFormat` in `76575afbfb` on 2026-10-27.

The row clears when that point's count reads 118. The correction is docs-only, so it
does not move the candidate. The test that holds the count is named
`test_the_frozen_rows_are_the_117_names_the_promise_reaches`, but it asserts
`FROZEN_COUNT`, which is 118, and it passed under section 2.2. Its name is no promise,
so it does not hold the row red and need not change before the cut. Renaming it is
test code, and would move the candidate. This record changes no spec, so it makes
neither change. The count is a fact to restate, not a choice, so it is not put to the
owner.

## 6. What 1.0 ends

Until `1.0`, a surface could change without an alias where carrying the alias cost
more than the rename. That licence is [§REQ-backwards-compatibility.4](../requirements/REQ-backwards-compatibility.md#4-what-was-never-a-promise), and it is the one
thing that expires at `1.0`. From the `1.0.0` cut on, the deprecation path of
[§REQ-backwards-compatibility.2](../requirements/REQ-backwards-compatibility.md#2-the-deprecation-path) is the default for every change to the user-visible surface
[§REQ-backwards-compatibility.1](../requirements/REQ-backwards-compatibility.md#1-what-is-covered) covers: the new form ships beside the old with a warning,
and the old form goes no earlier than the next release. For the embedding surface
[§FS-distribution.3.1.2](../functional-spec/FS-distribution.md#312-what-10-freezes) freezes, a `1.x` release may add a name, a field or a variant
and may deprecate a name, and only `2.0` removes or retypes a frozen name.

Two narrow routes stay open after `1.0`, as they were before it. A verdict may
still flip in the release that introduces the change, by the loud mechanical migration of
[§REQ-backwards-compatibility.3](../requirements/REQ-backwards-compatibility.md#3-loud-mechanical-migrations): the finding names the versions, the fix is one
documented command, and the release notes it. The managed block is its standing case,
which is why the row "Managed regions stay versioned and migrate loudly" holds
(section 3). And a verdict another requirement already forbade may still be corrected
under the five conditions of [§REQ-backwards-compatibility.5](../requirements/REQ-backwards-compatibility.md#5-correcting-a-verdict-another-requirement-forbids). The corrections made
by that route before `1.0`, #452 among them, are preserved. Neither route is a rename
licence: the correction route's own fifth condition says it cannot justify ordinary
tightening or removal, which still owe the deprecation path or a loud migration.

Every v1 config keeps loading with its meaning ([§FS-config.5.2](../functional-spec/FS-config.md#52-every-older-version-keeps-its-meaning)), and its caution names no deadline
([§FS-config.5.3](../functional-spec/FS-config.md#53-a-version-1-config-is-cautioned-and-never-retired)).
